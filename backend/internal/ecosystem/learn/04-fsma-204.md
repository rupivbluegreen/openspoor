---
title: FSMA 204 — the food traceability rule
tags: [comply, food, fsma]
---
21 CFR Part 1 Subpart S applies to foods on the FDA **Food Traceability List**. You record **Key Data Elements (KDEs)** at **Critical Tracking Events (CTEs)**: harvesting, cooling, initial packing, first land-based receiving, shipping, receiving and transformation.

The **traceability lot code (TLC)** is assigned at initial packing, first land-based receiving or transformation, and travels with the food. On request, FDA can ask for an **electronic sortable spreadsheet within 24 hours**; records are kept for **2 years**.

In Open Spoor: create lots with their TLC, record CTEs (shipping requires the reference document and recipient), record transformations (many inputs to one output lot), and export the spreadsheet from **Food → FSMA 204**, optionally for one lot and everything upstream and downstream of it. A recall traces forward through transformations to every customer.
