# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers via the contact in SECURITY.md. Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers may warn, suspend or ban participants who violate this code.
